Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

The short version: this website doesn't use cookies, doesn't run ads or tracking, and doesn't collect or store anything you type into it. If you contact us, it's through your own phone or email app, and we only use what you send to answer you.

This policy explains how Eli's Original Steaks & Subs ("Eli's", "we", "us") handles information when you visit this website. It covers this site only. Our restaurant at 901 Center St in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania, and outside services like Grubhub have their own practices.

What we collect

We don't ask you to create an account, and the site has no login, no newsletter signup and no online payment. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we never have.

  • Things you choose to send us. The "Big orders" form on our homepage doesn't send anything by itself. When you press the button, it opens your own email app with a message already written out. Nothing leaves your device until you hit send in that app. If you do send it, we'll receive your email address and whatever you wrote, like your name, phone number and order details. Same goes if you call us.
  • Basic technical logs. Our website host, Vercel, automatically processes standard request information (such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We don't use this to identify you.

How we use it

We use what you send us to answer your question, take your order and follow up about it. That's it. We don't add you to marketing lists unless you ask us to.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking scripts. Your browser may remember small things locally on its own, but we don't read or store them.

Outside services on this site

A few pieces of the site come from other companies. When your browser loads them, those companies may receive your IP address and basic browser information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps shows our location in the "Hours & Location" section. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts provides the lettering used on the site. See Google Fonts privacy information.
  • Vercel hosts the site. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

We also link to Grubhub, Facebook, Instagram and Yelp. Once you click over to those sites, their privacy policies apply, not ours. If you order through Grubhub, they handle your payment and delivery details.

How long we keep things

Emails and messages are kept only as long as we need them to handle your order or question, plus a reasonable time after in case you follow up. You can ask us to delete them any time.

Keeping it safe

The site is served only over an encrypted HTTPS connection, uses strict browser security settings, and doesn't load scripts from advertisers or data brokers. No website is perfectly secure, but we keep this one simple on purpose so there's very little to protect in the first place.

Kids

This site is meant for a general audience and isn't aimed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has sent us information, let us know and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what information we have from you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights under state privacy laws. We'll honor reasonable requests no matter where you're from. Just email or call us and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
