Accessibility

Last updated November 5, 2026

We want everybody to be able to check our menu and find us, however you get around the web. This site is built with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA in mind.

What we've done

  • High contrast text and clear, readable type sizes
  • Full keyboard navigation, with visible focus outlines and a skip to content link
  • Menu tabs that work with arrow keys and screen readers
  • Text descriptions for images and labeled form fields with clear error messages
  • Animations that switch off automatically if your device is set to reduce motion
  • A layout that works on phones, tablets and desktops, and that zooms without breaking

Something not working?

If anything on this site is hard to use, tell us and we'll fix it. And if you'd rather skip the website entirely, call us at 610-419-4506. We're happy to read you the menu and take your order over the phone.

You can also email elisoriginalsteakssubs@gmail.com.
