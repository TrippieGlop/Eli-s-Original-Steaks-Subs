Our story

Nothing fancy. Just done right.

Eli's is a neighborhood sub shop on Center Street in Bethlehem. The idea is simple: good bread, plenty of meat, and a grill that's always hot.

We make a lot of it ourselves. The potato chips, the pierogies and the tuna salad all come out of our kitchen, and the ribeye for The Original gets shaved right here. And if you want your steak a certain way, just say so. Special requests are kind of our thing.

About the goat. Every good sub shop needs a mascot, and ours happens to wear a chef hat and an apron that says G.O.A.T. We'll let you decide if he's earned it.
